## Internet

_Let they know about 2.4GHz, 5GHz and 6GHz_

- The 2.4 GHz band provides longer-range coverage but transmits data at slower speeds.

- The 5 GHz band offers less coverage but transmits at faster speeds than the 2.4 GHz band.

- The 6 GHz band provides the least coverage but transmits data at superfast speeds.
